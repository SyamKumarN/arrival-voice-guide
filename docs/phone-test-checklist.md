# Phone test checklist (one page)

Open https://arrival-voice.vercel.app in **Chrome on Android**. Allow the microphone when asked. Tap the mic for each line, then speak. Tick each box when the app does what the right-hand column says.

## Before you start
- [ ] The footer says **Live rates** and today's or yesterday's date.
- [ ] The **Hands-free** box is ticked.

## Hindi / Hinglish (default chip)
| Say | Expect |
|---|---|
| "dubai terminal teen, paise badalne hain" | Names the T3 landside exchange counters, highlights step 1 and speaks in Hindi. |
| *(wait)* | After it finishes speaking, the mic turns red by itself. It must **not** be red while the app is talking. |
| "आगे" | Step 2 (Travelex warning). |
| "नहीं मिल रहा" | Info desk / ATM advice, then "दोबारा या आगे". |
| "500 dollar ke satrah sau dirham de rahe hain" | Price card: 1,700 vs about 1,836, **7.4% कम**, "महँगा". |
| "स्टाफ से पूछना है" | Black card with **Arabic + English**, read aloud. Tap **बंद करें** to close it. |
| "मुझे सिम चाहिए" | SIM route (5 steps, passport tip). |
| "होटल जाने के लिए टैक्सी" | Taxi route (taxi signs, official queue). |
| "मौसम कैसा है" | "I can guide you to … exchange, SIM, taxi …" in Hindi, plus the command hint. |

## English chip
| Say | Expect |
|---|---|
| "I'm lost" | Asks which airport. |
| "Dubai", then "one" | Asks: passport counters, baggage belts, customs or arrivals hall? |
| "I can see baggage belts" | Jumps to step 2 of the T1 route. |
| "Bangkok, I want a SIM" | Polite "no step-by-step route here yet", arrivals-hall advice, and a **Thai + English** card opens. |
| "Singapore terminal 2, taxi" | Short safe answer and an **English-only** card. |
| "Delhi terminal 3, change money" | Route with the note "Details still being checked: ask at the info desk". |

## Telugu chip
| Say | Expect |
|---|---|
| "దుబాయ్ టెర్మినల్ 1 డబ్బు మార్చాలి" | Al Ansari route in Telugu. If there's no spoken voice, a "no voice" note appears and the text is still on screen. |
| "సిమ్ కావాలి" | SIM route. |
| "స్టాఫ్‌ని అడగాలి" | Staff card (Arabic + English for Dubai). |
| "నేను ఎక్కడున్నానో తెలియదు" | Starts the "what do you see?" question. |

## Screen checks
- [ ] On the Dubai T3 route, the yellow and red warning cards under the steps have **no number badges over the text**.
- [ ] Nothing runs off the side of the screen. The bottom buttons don't cover the highlighted step.
- [ ] Turn the phone sideways and back: the layout still fits.

**Write down:** your phone model, Android and Chrome versions, and anything misheard (exactly what the app showed under "You said").
