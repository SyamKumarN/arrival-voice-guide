# Phone test checklist

Open https://arrival-voice.vercel.app in **Chrome on Android** and allow the microphone. Tap the mic, speak, and tick the box when the app does what the right-hand column says. Every row can also be tested by typing in the box or tapping the buttons.

## 1. Before you fly (on home Wi-Fi)
- [ ] The footer shows **Live rates** and a recent date.
- [ ] Tap **Save for my trip**. A panel says it was saved, with the date and the language-help tip. If Chrome offers **Add to home screen**, add it.
- [ ] Turn on **flight mode**, reopen the app: it loads, the footer says **Rates saved on this phone**, and the route buttons work. Tapping the mic says **"Voice needs internet. Connect to airport Wi-Fi or use the buttons."** Turn flight mode off again.

## 2. Hindi / Hinglish (default chip)
| Say | Expect |
|---|---|
| "dubai terminal teen, paise badalne hain" | **"दुबई, टर्मिनल 3। क्या यह सही है?"** with **हाँ / नहीं** buttons. |
| "हाँ" | Route starts. The mic is **not** red while the app talks, and turns red after it finishes. |
| "आगे" → "नहीं मिल रहा" | Step 2, then info desk / ATM advice. |
| "काउंटर बंद है" | Next counter or airport ATM, choose local currency. The route stays. |
| "500 dollar ke satrah sau dirham de rahe hain" | Price card: 7.4% कम, "महँगा". |
| "आगे" until the last step | The last step says to **count the money and ask for a receipt**. Then **3 money tips**, one at a time (आगे / दोबारा). |
| "धोखे बताइए" | 5 traps, one at a time. |
| "टॉयलेट कहाँ है" | "मैं आपकी एयरपोर्ट अराइवल गाइड हूँ…", then a card in **Arabic + English** ("where is the toilet?"). |
| "मुझे सिम चाहिए" / "होटल जाने के लिए टैक्सी" | Confirm, then a SIM route, or a taxi route with the step "ignore people offering rides". |

## 3. English chip
| Say | Expect |
|---|---|
| "I'm lost" → "Dubai" → "one" → "yes" | Asks what you see. "baggage belts" jumps to step 2. |
| "Dubai terminal 3" → "no" | Asks for the airport again. |
| "I have a connecting flight" | Don't exit; follow the Transfer signs. |
| "how much money should I change" | First day only: taxi, SIM, a meal. |
| "the card machine asks rupees or dirhams" | Always choose the local currency. |
| "I have a forex card" / "credit card" / "debit card" | The matching card advice. |
| "where is the ATM" | 4 ATM tips, one at a time. |
| "my driver is picking me up" | 5 taxi and pickup tips (official pickup point, OTPs, luggage, small cash). |
| "Bangkok, I want a SIM" | Short safe answer and a **Thai + English** card. |
| "Delhi terminal 3, change money" → "yes" | EbixCash route, "Last checked: October 2026". "Delhi terminal 2" asks again: T1 or T3. |
| "Dubai, change money" | The terminal question says Emirates uses Terminal 3. |
| "Bengaluru terminal 1, change money" → "yes" | Ends with: no counter in T1, use an ATM or Terminal 2. |
| "where is the metro" | Safe answer and a staff card. Nothing invented. |

## 5. Screen and settings
- [ ] Tick **Speak slower**: the next reply is clearly slower.
- [ ] On the Dubai T3 route, the warning cards have **no number badges over the text**.
- [ ] Nothing runs off the side of the screen. The bottom buttons don't cover the highlighted step. It still fits when you rotate the phone.

**Write down:** your phone model, Android and Chrome versions, and anything misheard (exactly what the app showed under "You said").
